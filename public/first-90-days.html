LEADERSHIP PLAN

First 90 days: listen, focus, prove.

A starting point, not a script. The sequence should respond to the organization’s priorities, people, risk, and existing capability.

01—30

Understand the system

Listen to leaders and practitioners. Map priorities, workflows, platforms, data, controls, and current AI experiments. Agree on decision rights and a baseline.

Leave with: a shared view of the work, constraints, owners, and how success will be judged.

31—60

Choose a bounded opportunity

Co-design one or two opportunities with accountable owners. Test value, feasibility, data readiness, human oversight, and a safe path to learn.

Leave with: a small, owned experiment and clear measures, guardrails, and stop conditions.

61—90

Prove and prepare to scale

Review evidence with users and sponsors. Decide to improve, expand, pause, or stop. Capture reusable measures and the next choices.

Leave with: an evidence-based next decision and a practical path for the work that earns it.
