AN ADAPTABLE STARTING POINT

First 90 days:
listen, focus, prove.

This is an illustrative, organization-adaptable plan—not a promise of predetermined outcomes. The sequence changes with people, strategy, risk, and existing capability.

01—30

Understand the system

Listen to leaders and practitioners. Map priorities, workflows, platforms, data, controls, and current AI experiments. Agree on decision rights and a shared baseline.

31—60

Choose a bounded opportunity

Co-design one or two opportunities with accountable owners. Test value, feasibility, data readiness, human oversight, and a safe path to learn.

61—90

Prove and prepare to scale

Review evidence with users and sponsors. Decide to improve, expand, pause, or stop. Capture reusable guardrails, measures, and the next portfolio choices.

04 / ADOPTION

Adaptable change approach
Open the adoption sequence

Listen to affected teams; co-design the workflow; practice with realistic cases; collect friction and trust signals; adapt training and support; share what changed and what did not.

05 / VALUE

Measure what changed

Illustrative measures: task time, rework, user confidence, exception rate, capacity redeployed, and verified cost. A time saving is not a financial return until the organization validates the conversion and baseline.
