# Can an AI answer survive a source check? Duolingo example

**Review date:** November 24, 2026  
**Source basis:** Public company and SEC materials  
**Status:** First worked example in an independent source-checking research project; prepared with AI assistance. No product test, employer engagement, or investment recommendation.

## Question

What does Duolingo's public reporting say about AI-enabled speaking practice, user activity, and the cost of expanding AI features?

## Why I chose this example

The public letter discusses both AI-enabled speaking practice and the cost of expanding AI features. That makes it possible to examine reported product activity alongside operating trade-offs while keeping the limits of the evidence visible.

## Primary sources

- [Q3 2026 shareholder letter (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm), filed November 3, 2026; metrics table, speaking-practice update, Video Call plans, and Q4 guidance.
- [Q3 2026 shareholder letter (Duolingo Investor Relations PDF)](https://investors.duolingo.com/static-files/e1ee336e-b024-486f-875c-afd722653067), company-hosted copy.
- [2025 Form 10-K (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012494/duol-20251231.htm), company description and annual disclosures.

## Directly supported facts

- The shareholder letter reports Q3 2026 average daily active users of 64.9 million versus 50.5 million in Q3 2025 (+29% year over year).
- It reports 13.1 million paid subscribers at quarter end versus 11.5 million a year earlier (+14%).
- It reports Q3 2026 revenue of $309.0 million versus $271.7 million (+14%).
- Management says the share of exercises answerable by speaking doubled since the beginning of 2026.
- Management describes AI-powered Video Call and says it expects to expand it to all Super subscribers by year end. This is a forward-looking plan.
- Management's Q4 2026 guidance expects gross margin of 72%, down 90 basis points sequentially as AI-feature rollout expands. This is company guidance, not an actual Q4 result.

## Interpretation

The public materials describe a product opportunity and an operating-cost question at the same time. More realistic speaking practice may help learners, while AI inference costs and tier access shape how broadly it can be offered. A useful evaluation should pair learner progress and repeated speaking practice with cost and access measures. Daily activity is not, by itself, evidence of learning.

## Unknowns

The public sources reviewed here do not establish causal learning outcomes from Video Call, usage differences between free and paid learners, unit costs by feature, or whether lower per-unit AI costs offset total expansion costs. This exercise has no access to internal data and does not independently verify company metrics.

## Limits

This brief is a public-source reading sample, not a product evaluation or stock recommendation. The figures are attributed to company materials; guidance may change. No learner testing, deployment, or business result is claimed.
